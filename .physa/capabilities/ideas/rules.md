Agents can draft pitches. Only a person decides to build one.
Because: agents never make product decisions.

### The circuit breaker never extends
`breaker-holds` · from docs/vision.md
A bet out of appetite stops and goes back to shaping. It is never extended automatically.
Because: an appetite that stretches is an estimate in disguise.

### No backlog
`no-backlog` · from docs/vision.md
Ideas nobody adds to wither. Nothing is kept just because it was written down.
Because: backlogs become graveyards that cost attention to ignore.

### Ideas stay out of the map
`ideas-not-in-map` · from docs/architecture/01-map-and-slice.md
Ideas, pitches and bets live in the engine's store and the hosted layer. When a pitch is bet on, its rabbit holes and no-gos move into the map as rules or rejected options.
Because: work is not knowledge; Rhiza's model drowned in run records.
