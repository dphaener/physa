### Shape Up as a starting point
`shape-up-start` · decided by dphaener, in their words · 2026-11-19 · from docs/vision.md
Physa borrows pitches, appetites, bets and circuit breakers from Shape Up, and replaces whatever does not fit work built by agents: scope means a pitch's breadth and is set during shaping, appetites are tokens, and hills and countable scopes are gone.
Because: its ideas were about judgment, not about how slow building was.
Rejected:
- Six-week cycles and cooldown: sized to human building speed.
- Adopting it whole: it is a jumping-off point, not a template.

### An idea is a thin tube from the start
`idea-is-tube` · decided by dphaener, in their words · 2026-11-19 · from docs/vision.md
An idea exists the moment a person wants to try something, and belongs to them. A signal is required by shaping, not before.
Because: the PM's hunch, the designer's layout and the developer's architecture are each already a feeler.
Rejected:
- Ideas waiting in a queue until they earn a tube.
- "Probe" as the name for an idea in use.

### Ideas live only in the local store for now
`ideas-local-store` · decided by dphaener, choosing an option · 2026-11-21 · 319dc9d
Ideas, pitches and bets live only in the repo's local store, which is never committed. Until the hosted layer exists they are gone on a re-clone and invisible on a second machine or to a teammate.
Because: the person chose the local store alone over the other options offered.
Rejected:
- The store, mirrored to git notes: team digests on notes are already planned for a later step.
- Committed files in the repo: ideas stay out of the map, since work is not knowledge.
