# The map

This is the product's map: why the product is the way it is.

- Read `product.md` first. Its rules apply to every change.
- Each file in `capabilities/` is one promise the product makes, with its rules, decisions and open questions. Read the ones related to your task.
- `glossary.md` holds the words to use, and the words to avoid.

Anchor each commit to the capability it serves with a trailer: `Capability: <file name>`.
